# Search History

*What does a year of searching say about me?* **A year of my Google data, asked like a search bar.** Type "suhani is…" and it autocompletes into what the data found: when I sleep, what I search at 3 AM, which day I get the most done, and how I went from my first line of Python to shipping my own products.

**→ [suhxnitiwari.github.io/search-history](https://suhxnitiwari.github.io/search-history/)**

105,092 pages · 19,626 searches · 1,974 videos · 297 files · two Google accounts · Oct 2025 – Sep 2026

The follow-up to [Heavy Rotation](https://suhxnitiwari.github.io/listening-galaxy/), my Spotify galaxy.

## What it answers

- **a builder:** the dated path from a product pitch, to my first Python notebook, to React, to my own data products
- **a night owl:** my typical offline window for each night of the week
- **most productive on thursdays:** what I'm most likely doing each day
- **in beauty mode at 3 am:** my searches hour by hour, with "Play my day"
- **dreaming of nyc**, **a marketer**, **a networker**, **up all night for what matters**, **an artist**, **a baker**, **a published author**, **an explorer**, **always misspelling austin**

Misspell an answer and it asks, *Did you mean…?*

## Data and privacy

Two Google Takeout exports (Chrome, YouTube, Drive file titles and dates). Nothing is shown unless it's on a hand-written allowlist; everything else is dropped. No raw data, search text beyond the examples on the page, file contents, store names, company names or personal documents are in this repo. "Sleep" is the longest stretch each night with no activity across my laptop and phone, not measured sleep.

## Status

A working prototype: the numbers come from my analysis and are written into the page. The pipeline that generates them is next.

Built with HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript, no framework. Photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), credited on each card. Not affiliated with Google.
